HOW IT WORKS

Deterministic, not generative

No inpainting model guesses what should be under the sparkle. The tool uses calibrated alpha maps for known Gemini watermark profiles and reverses the original blend mathematically.

01

Detect

Known watermark sizes and margins are scored against the bottom-right region. A fallback scan runs only when needed.

02

Reverse blend

The calibrated alpha map is applied to reconstruct the RGB values that existed before the visible white sparkle was blended in.

03

Download

The cleaned pixels are encoded in your browser. You preview the result and download it without sending the source image to a server.

LIMITS & PRIVACY

What this tool does — and doesn’t

Does it upload my image?

No. Image decoding, pixel processing and encoding all happen in the current browser tab. The page only loads the open-source remover data shipped with this project.

Does it remove SynthID or other invisible provenance signals?

No. This project targets the visible Gemini sparkle watermark only. It does not claim to remove invisible watermarking, metadata-based provenance systems or SynthID.

Will it modify an image when detection is uncertain?

No. The fallback scanner uses confidence thresholds. If a known profile cannot be identified confidently, the web tool stops and leaves your image untouched.

Is this affiliated with Google or Gemini?

No. This is an independent open-source utility. Gemini and related product names are trademarks of their respective owners.
