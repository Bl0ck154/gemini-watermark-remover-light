How it works

Analyze. Remove. Clean.

Video Light analyzes the clip, removes the visible mark from every frame, cleans the remaining compression halo, and rebuilds the video locally.

  1. 1
    Analyze

    The watermark is located and checked across the clip before processing starts.

  2. 2
    Remove

    The visible mark is removed from every frame rather than only from a few sampled frames.

  3. 3
    Clean & rebuild

    High-quality cleanup targets the remaining halo, then the finished MP4 is rebuilt locally in your browser.

Current scope

Local first.
Fail closed.

Video Light targets visible Gemini/Veo diamond-style marks. If the mark cannot be located safely, processing stops instead of modifying a random part of the video. Your clip is never uploaded.
