Your video stays on this device. Video processing requires a browser with WebCodecs H.264 support; current Chrome/Edge are the safest choice.

What v1 does

Lightweight first. Quality-aware.

Multi-frame detection

Samples frames across the clip instead of trusting frame zero, so fades and busy scenes are less likely to fool detection.

Stable geometry

Locks one watermark position for the clip and applies confidence checks per frame to reduce flicker and accidental damage.

Soft residual cleanup

A small local edge cleanup reduces H.264 ringing without shipping a neural model in the first release.

Known limit

Some heavily compressed or unusual clips can still leave a faint residual. Enhanced WebGPU/WASM cleanup is planned separately.
